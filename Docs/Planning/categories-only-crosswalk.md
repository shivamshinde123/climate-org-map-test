## Category sources

This document compares categories from:

- IRS EO BMF
- ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer
- Climate Org Mapping

ProPublica uses the same IRS nonprofit categories. Therefore, the IRS and
ProPublica category names are normally identical.

## Proposed Organization Type matches

The IRS and ProPublica nonprofit categories do not reliably distinguish every
Climate Org Mapping Organization Type.

| IRS or ProPublica category information | Proposed Organization Type |
|---|---|
| College or university is explicitly identified | Academic |
| Business or commercial organization is explicitly identified | Business |
| Alliance, network, partnership, or coalition is explicitly identified | Coalition |
| Government-affiliated organization is explicitly identified | Municipal / Government-affiliated |
| Municipal government itself is explicitly identified | Municipality |
| Nonprofit or grassroots organization is explicitly identified, and no more specific type applies | Nonprofit / Grassroots |
| Only nonprofit tax status is known | Unknown |
| Information is missing or conflicting | Unknown |

## Proposed Resilience matches

| Organization focus | Proposed Resilience |
|---|---|
| Preparedness, flood protection, heat resilience, climate-risk reduction, or adaptation | Adaptation |
| Emissions reduction, clean energy, renewable energy, decarbonization, or energy efficiency | Mitigation |
| Clearly performs both adaptation and mitigation work | Both |
| General environmental or conservation work without a stated climate strategy | Unknown |

Of the selected IRS and ProPublica categories, C35 is the only category proposed
as a direct Mitigation candidate. Conservation alone does not automatically mean
Adaptation.
